How to be a good listener
  • Listen first. Reflect back what you hear, like "That sounds really hard."
  • Do not rush to fix or judge.
  • Do not give medical, legal or religious rulings. Point to a qualified person.
  • Never ask for personal details, photos or money.
  • If someone may harm themselves, encourage them to contact emergency services or a helpline.
  • End the session if you feel drained. Your wellbeing matters too.
